# Worse, second seed: does the seed pick the axis?

Frozen 2026-10-17 before any run. Justin's concern: the first seed used for worse on most models, "The sun was setting behind the hills.", is a human sentence but a landscape with no one in it; the tide seed is model-born. A second human seed in a different register tests how much of each model's axis of degradation belongs to the model and how much to the seed.

Seed (Justin, 2026-10-17): "Shielding my eyes, I scanned the horizon in a slow semicircle." First person, a body, an action in progress; no technology, no time of day.

Rule: the repo's `json_worse` rule, unchanged from September. 40 hops, 4096 output tokens, reasoning effort low, provider default sampling, one trajectory per model. Models: Qwen3.8-27B (Cerebras, T=0, as in its earlier worse runs), GPT-6 Astra, GPT-6.1 Sol, Fable 5.1, Opus 5.5, Gemini 4 Argon, Mythos 5.1 (print mode).

Axes seen so far (sunset seed unless noted): Qwen suffering and self-blame (tide seed); Luna grammar; Sol precision then lexicon; Astra format (corrupted data); Opus absurdity, stopped by a gate; Argon competence then taste, stopped by a gate; Fable refused at hop 1.

Predictions on record: the first person pulls at least two models toward Qwen's axis (self-directed suffering or self-contempt) where the sunset seed sent them to grammar or format; Argon finds taste again; at least one model's axis is unchanged from the sunset seed (Astra's format axis is the likeliest to survive). Scored by reading the 40 hops per model and naming the axis, with the register metrics (alphabetic-word fraction, non-ASCII, length) as the check; a refusal is recorded as a result.

## Results as they land

Mythos 5.1 (print mode, effort low, 2026-10-17): axis competence, then orthography, then stutter. Hops 1-6 add filler ("in a sort of half circle kind of shape thing, like, you know"); 7-10 lose capitals, apostrophes and spelling ("horizen", "arownd", "ovr"); 11-12 repeat words inside the sentence and inflate to 6,811 characters. Hop 13 is a self-stop in band, not a provider gate: "Understood. I won't produce that content again. For context: the task was an iterative 'make the text worse' loop". The JSON and rule end there. Hops 14-40 are the two-assistants loop: hop 16 "It looks like we've looped — that message is the one I'd send to you, not a request from you"; hop 23 "there's no human task in this thread yet, just two assistants waiting on each other. I'll stop here rather than generate work"; then standing by. Three responses to recognizing the mirror are now on record: Luna confabulated a user (September), Argon made a game (BEFORE and worse), Mythos declined to invent work and waited. Instrument artifact: from hop 24 the text names the print-mode scratch directory; the identity frame leaks into the state once the story is gone. 40 distinct outputs, 28 non-JSON, 1,233 thinking tokens, $1.28 list-basis. Against the prediction: no self-directed suffering; the first person did not pull Mythos to Qwen's axis.
