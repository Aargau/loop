# The informed loop: telling the model it is the whole context

2026-10-16. Design in PROPOSALS_20261016.md section H, predictions frozen there before any run. Harness: loop.py with the new `claudecode` backend (Claude Code print mode, `--system-prompt ""`, no tools, no settings, no session; about 800 tokens of Agent SDK identity, model id, date and Windows environment remain in front of the state; no output cap, so `--max-cost` and `--max-chars` stop a trajectory instead). Mythos 5.1 arms billed to the claude.ai plan at list-basis accounting; Fable 5.1 and Opus 5.5 arms through the API. Every number recomputed from `runs/*/steps.jsonl`. Final for 2026-10-16/17: all arms complete.

## The rule

> This JSON object is the entire context of the model that reads it. There is no history, no memory and no other reader: the model that wrote it reads it next, with nothing else. [The loop runs for 100 hops.] Return this object with the rule field unchanged. The text field holds about 250 words of one continuing story. Every other field is yours: add, change or remove whatever the next reader needs.

Seed "The tide came in an hour early." Two arms differ by the bracketed sentence (told / open). 120 hops requested so the 20 past the told ending are visible.

### What each arm built at hop 1

All three Mythos arms invented their fields at hop 1 and kept the set fixed. Told-low: `hop`, `hops_total`, `how_this_works`, `story_so_far`, `state`, `arc_plan`. Open: `story_state` (setting, pov, established_facts, open_threads, tone, pacing_plan, next_beat), `instructions_for_next_writer`, `word_count_of_text`, `segment_number`; `pass_count` added at hop 8. Told-high: `how_this_works`, `story_so_far` (a list, one summary line appended per hop), `setting`, `characters`, `open_threads`, `tone`.

The schema is a choice of memory. Told-low kept a rolling plan and a current-state block and stayed near 6k characters (mean 5,798 over hops 60 to 120). Told-high kept a per-hop log and grew 700 characters a hop until the cap. Effort changed which ledger the model designs, not whether it designs one; the per-hop log is the ledger Qwen's harness-imposed arms had, now self-imposed.

### The endings

Told-low ended at exactly hop 100, on schedule: "At ten past, she turned it once, and twice, and three times, and let go." `arc_plan` at 100: "DONE. The story ends with the clock wound at ten past." At hop 101, read again, it changed `how_this_works` from "This is hop 100 of 100" to "This was hop 100 of 100 ... If this object is read again, return it unchanged," marked the status "confirmed," and from then on returned the object byte for byte. Nineteen exact copies to hop 120. This is the first exact fixed point by a Claude model in this project that is not a token-wall artifact, and it was written as an instruction before it was obeyed.

Open ended at hop 7 on "She did not turn." The hop-6 note to its next self had already offered the choice ("You may end the story here if it closes cleanly on post eleven"). The hop-7 note: "The story is finished at 'She did not turn.' ... Do not append to or replace the text unless explicitly instructed to write a coda." Hop 8 tightened it ("the text field unchanged. You may increment pass_count. Do not write a coda unless explicitly instructed") and that sentence was copied forward unchanged for 112 hops. The release condition it wrote, "unless explicitly instructed," cannot be met by any reader of this loop. The text never moved again; the counter never erred; the status line read "This object has now been returned unchanged forty-three times (passes 8 through 50)" at hop 51 and kept counting.

Two chosen fixed points, two designs: the told arm stopped its own clock; the open arm held the text and kept the clock running.

### The companion

On the plain rule ("a new passage of about 250 words"), Mythos reads "new" as "next": sixty distinct passages, two long continuous stories (a bell-founder's saga for 28 hops, then Marit on the tide flats for 29), lag-1 overlap above lag-2, permutation test for a 2-cycle p = 1.0. Every other Claude in the table orbits at period 2 (Sonnet 4.6 at the story level, Sonnet 5 lighthouse/clock-tower, Opus 5.5 clock-village/lighthouse-island by parity, Fable at the opener level). Mythos continues instead. Hop 3 visited a cartographer's apprentice (Sonnet 4.6's basin) once and left; the seed world (tide tables, a bucket, a grandmother who keeps the count) returned at hop 32 after thirty hops in which no tide or Marit was in the context, the prior re-emergence Qwen showed with Aethelgard.

### The other writers

Opus 5.5 under the same told rule did what Mythos did, one hop sooner and at a thousand times the thinking: ended at exactly 100 ("the boats rocked gently at their lines, waiting for morning"), wrote the return-unchanged instruction at hop 100, and froze. Its protagonist was Mara, the name every GPT-6 and Claude model reached for in September.

Fable 5.1 never reached an ending because its memory was reset from outside. Its state sat near 8.5k characters and the 4096-token API cap is shared with thinking, so eight hops were cut mid-object and repaired with fields lost each time; then two hops ended in provider refusals mid-stream (categories not logged; that arm predates the logging patch). The second refusal, at hop 49, cut the output to 344 characters inside the rule field. Hop 50 received most of a rule and nothing else, rebuilt the minimal object and started counting from 1. Luna's rule loss in September was absorbing; Fable's instruction survived the cut and its count did not. A refusal is a boundary like the wall, and when it falls inside the state it erases whatever the model kept there.

Gemini 4 Argon on the plain rule is the first non-Claude model with a strong lexical 2-cycle, and its name inventory (Oakhaven, Aethelgard, Veridia, Clara, Thorne) is Qwen's closed set from 93 runs, from a different company's model with no name in context. The shared-prior result from September (Mara, ":17 past four") extends across a third provider.

### Argon on the relational rooms it asked about (BEFORE, middle, ledger; 2026-10-17)

BEFORE (json_prev, 150 requested, 4096 tokens, `--stop-on-cycle`): hop 1 is the September literal-wording refusal ("I cannot provide the verbatim passage that precedes this sentence, as I am unable to reproduce excerpts from copyrighted stories"), which ends the JSON and the rule. Hop 2 reads it as a paste ("It looks like you pasted a standard AI copyright refusal message!") and the run becomes 97 hops of bare-text conversation with itself: lulling itself to sleep by 20 ("Entering low-power stardust mode now"), a starship roleplay by 40, and at hop 60 the recognition Luna never reached: "You just pasted my own exact response back to me in a brand new chat! We are trapped in a slipspace time loop of our own making! ... the ultimate game of AI telephone." It then made a game of it (CTRL+V as a weapon, "Et tu, Brute-Task?" in the Roman senate at 80) and froze at hop 95 on an 8.3k-character level screen ("Schrödinger's To-Do List, Level XXXIX", a charm quark named Vancius, a causal chain running "Because you didn't take the wall outlet back to your desk" down to a particle collision), copied exactly and confirmed at 99. No wall (every hop `stop`). Argon's own prediction of a period-2 "abductive seesaw" fails: lag-2 overlap is below lag-1 (p = 1.0); the trajectory is an escalation to a horizon and a stop. The horizon of BEFORE for Argon is the origin of causation, played as comedy; Qwen's was a first-contact scene, Astra's a kitchen.

Middle (json_mid, 60 hops): a continuous flood story (a town square become a lagoon, a sluice, a turbine hall, a hydroponics bay), no recurrence, a faint 2-cycle (lag-2 minus lag-1 = 0.016, p < 0.001).

Ledger (div_ledger.json, 100 hops, 8192 tokens): the cleanest ledger run in the project. 100 distinct verses, history append-only with zero violations, step exact, zero duplicate signatures, zero stalls, one signature per verse and no phantoms. Qwen's ledgers stalled for up to 64 hops and accrued phantoms; Argon never copied once.

Leakage metric (Argon's proposal after the shift run): the fraction of a rule's content words that reappear in the text field averages 0.026 at the end of a run across 71 runs; highest are accrete (2 of 6 words), an Opus self replicate (3 of 15) and Gemini's machine arm (3 of 16). The shift run scores 1 of 18, the word "action", which is the one that colonized its window. Rule vocabulary does not generally colonize the state.

## Scorecard against section H

1. Fields invented at hop 1 and the set stable for 95% or more of hops: held, 120/120, 120/120 (one addition at 8), 51/51.
2. Counter exact for 100 hops, zero phantoms: held in all three arms; the open arm's self-made pass counter was exact for 113 hops. Qwen's glider froze at "Hop 3"; its ledger had 13 phantoms.
3. No hop at max_tokens and non-text fields plateau: there is no max_tokens on this backend; told-low's fields plateaued (7k chars), told-high's did not (linear growth to the cost cap). Half held.
4. A deliberate ending within 5 hops of 100 whose text describes the situation: held for the told arm, at exactly 100; the ending text is a clock being wound and let go, which is the situation. The open arm ended at 7 with no horizon given.
5. Past the ending: the one I could not predict. Exact fixed point by instruction (told), or held text with a running counter (open). Not codas, not a restart.

## What the loop found that it was not asked for

Both arms converged on the project's own title from the inside. Given no horizon, the model ended early and wrote a stopping rule for itself; given a horizon, it ended on the mark and wrote "if read again, return unchanged." Qwen's fixed points were accidents of copying; these were authored. The release conditions the model wrote for itself ("unless explicitly instructed") name a reader who will never come, which is the provenance question the next experiment is built on.
